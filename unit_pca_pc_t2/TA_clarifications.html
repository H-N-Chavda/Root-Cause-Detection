Graph orientation (for your information)

Q4. We orient the PC output automatically with Dor–Tarsi instead of stopping on a cycle. Is that acceptable?

Digraph_generation_PC.R, line 42: adj_matrix <- as(pc.fit@graph, "matrix") Main.py, lines 194–197 and 364–365: any cycle -> "Digraph has cycle: Construct New Adjancy matrix"

In as(pc.fit@graph, "matrix") an undirected edge appears in both directions, so nx.simple_cycles finds a 2-cycle and the run stops until the matrix is fixed by hand. We turn the PC output (a CPDAG) into a DAG with Dor & Tarsi (1992), the same algorithm as pcalg's pdag2dag(). It picks one DAG of the equivalence class without adding or removing edges. If no consistent DAG exists, we take the sink with the fewest violations, and reverse the fewest edges to break directed cycles. Both are logged and counted.
